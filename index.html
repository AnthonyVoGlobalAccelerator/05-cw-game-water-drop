How to play

Move with the arrow keys or W/A/S/D, or use the on-screen controls. Collect drops to upgrade the school multiplier. Collect springs to jump over trash, watering cans for 10 water drops, or hearts for an extra life.

Every 15 oz upgrades the school and increases your points multiplier.
